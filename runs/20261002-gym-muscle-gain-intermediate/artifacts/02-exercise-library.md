## Summary
25 exercises were selected from the wger database for an intermediate client training for muscle gain with only a barbell, a flat bench and dumbbells. They cover squat (2), hinge (3), lunge (3), horizontal push (2), vertical push (2), horizontal pull (4), vertical pull (2, dumbbell-only substitutes), core (2), carry (1) and isolation work for shoulders, arms and calves (4). Every exercise was confirmed with the wger server and uses only Barbell, Bench or Dumbbell. The client has no squat rack or stands, no bench uprights, no incline bench, no pull-up bar and no cable machine. Plainly stated: no rack is available. Barbell squats, front squats, barbell bench press and barbell overhead press were therefore removed, and all squat-pattern options are dumbbell-based. Barbell options that remain are loaded from the floor (Romanian deadlift, bent-over row, Pendlay row) or rolled onto the hips from the floor (hip thrust). True vertical pulls are not available, so the dumbbell pullover and lat pull are the closest lat-focused options. The seated dumbbell shoulder press is described with a near-vertical backrest, which a flat bench lacks, so it should be performed standing or seated on the flat bench with a braced trunk.
