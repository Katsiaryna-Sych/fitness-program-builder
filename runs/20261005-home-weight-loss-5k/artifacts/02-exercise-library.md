## Summary
27 exercises were selected from the wger database across 10 movement patterns (squat, hinge, lunge, horizontal push, vertical push, horizontal pull, core, carry, conditioning, mobility/knee-hip stability). They use only the client's equipment: dumbbells up to 10 kg, resistance bands and bodyweight. The client is a 52-year-old beginner with fat-loss and 5 km running goals and mild patellofemoral knee pain. Selection is conservative: low-impact options, box/partial-range squats, reverse (not forward) lunges, and band knee and hip stability work. No bench, pull-up bar or machine is required. No vertical pull is included because it needs a pull-up bar, so band pull-aparts and rows cover upper-back work. Detailed contraindications are left to the safety review. The revised session-duration exception (week-9 and week-10 Day C runs may last up to about 60 min instead of 55 min) does not change equipment, constraints or goals, so the library is unchanged from the previous attempt.
