## Findings
1. **Advisory, G10 wording, program (no re-run required):** the rest rationale says "Rest under 90 s for muscular-endurance and fat-loss work at light-moderate loads (ACSM 2009 progression models)".
   - The ACSM abstract ties the < 90 s rest to "light to moderate loads (40-60% of 1 RM)" and "high repetitions (>15)". It says nothing about fat loss.
   - For 8-12 RM hypertrophy-type work it gives "1- to 2-min rest periods".
   - The 45-s rest after the 10-12-rep main lifts is therefore the program's interpretation, not a direct prescription. It is a reasonable interpretation at RPE 6-7, and the program says its aim is lean-mass retention, not maximal hypertrophy.
   - If the program step is re-run for any other reason: drop "and fat-loss", and label the 45-s main-lift rest as "short-rest interpretation of the ACSM muscular-endurance guidance at submaximal RPE 6-7".
   - The shorter rests do not conflict with the safety guidelines, which have no rest rule. The pain and RPE limits still apply.
