# Clarifications

## Round 1
- Q: What is your current experience level with exercise and running? — A: Beginner, regular walking only
- Q: How many weeks should the program last? — A: 10 weeks

## Round 2
- Q: A non-stop 5 km in week 10 fits the 45-min limit only at 8.3 min/km or faster and needs a big final-week jump (28 → 41.5 min continuous). How should this be handled? — A: Allow one longer run: keep 10 weeks; the final 5 km session (week 10 Day C) and the week-9 long run (Day C) may last up to about 55 min. All other sessions stay at 45 min max.

## Round 3
- Q: With 5-min walking warm-up and cool-down, a 55-min long run allows only ~45 min of continuous jogging (5 km would need ~9 min/km; expected beginner pace ~9.6 min/km). How should the week-10 run work? — A: Allow up to ~60 min: the week-9 and week-10 Day C long runs may last up to about 60 min (5 min walk warm-up + up to ~50 min continuous jog + 5 min walk cool-down), so 5 km fits at ~10 min/km. Every other session stays at 45 min max. This replaces the ~55 min exception from Round 2.
