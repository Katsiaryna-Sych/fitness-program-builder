## Findings
1. **G7, owner cardio (downstream program, progress): the primary goal cannot be delivered within the fixed constraints.** A complete beginner must complete 42.2 km in 4 weeks on 2 sessions of at most 20 min. The maximum training time is 160 min in total. The prescription reaches 9 min of jogging per session and about 12 to 20 km cumulative. The sources the artifacts cite and that were verified here require 3 to 6 months of base running 4x/week plus a 16 to 20 week block. Raising cardio volume or intensity to chase the goal would break the 20-min limit (G2), the cited ≤ 10% progression guidance, and beginner safety (G4). Removing the honest disclaimer would only hide the gap. **No step re-run can fix this.** Re-running cardio or program would use up retry attempts and produce the same result. Fix: requirements change only, confirmed by the client. Any one of these options would make a passing prescription possible:
   - (a) Change the primary goal for this 4-week block to a reachable target, e.g. "jog 3 to 5 min continuously at talk-test pace" or "first steps toward a 5 km". The marathon stays the long-term goal.
   - (b) Extend the timeline to at least 16 to 20 weeks after a 3 to 6 month base.
   - (c) Increase the frequency and session length toward at least 4 runs per week with longer runs.
2. **G5, owner exercises (downstream program, progress): no upper-body muscle groups are trained.** The fix needs no extra minutes.
   - **exercises:** search wger for bodyweight-only (id 7) upper-body options. Push: knee or incline push-up. Pull/back with no equipment: e.g. Superman / prone back extension / prone Y-T raise. Add each with easier and harder substitutions.
   - **program:** swap rather than add, to keep the 3.75-min strength block and the 19.75-min week-3/4 totals. Suggested swaps:
     - In Session A, replace Plank with a knee/incline push-up. The push-up is also anti-extension core work.
     - In Session B, replace Bird Dog with a prone back-extension / Y-raise. This trains the upper back and spinal erectors.
   - **program:** update Weekly Volume so that push 1 = pull 1, and drop the "balanced at zero" wording.
   - **progress:** update the Progress Metrics and Training Log rows that name Plank and Bird Dog. The plank checkpoint test may stay as written.
3. Advisory, owner requirements (no gate fails): "Available equipment: none (bodyweight exercise), Gym mat" contradicts the client's "No equipment". Correct it if requirements are re-opened for Finding 1.
4. Advisory, owners cardio and program (no gate fails): the 10% rule is cited as a weekly limit, but the Runner's World page phrases it as "in the first week". Reword it as "about 10% (Runner's World guidance for starting volume)" or add a source that supports a weekly limit.
