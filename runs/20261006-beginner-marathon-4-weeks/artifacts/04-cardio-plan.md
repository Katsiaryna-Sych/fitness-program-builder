# Cardio Plan
> Run: 20261006-beginner-marathon-4-weeks · Step: cardio · Owner: cardio-planner · Attempt: 2

## Summary
The client's fixed limits (4 weeks, 2 days per week on Monday and Friday, at most 20 minutes per session, no equipment, any location) allow a conservative walk/jog base-building block of 24 to 30 cardio minutes per week, rising about 7 to 8 percent per week. This is well below the 150 minutes per week that WHO recommends for adults, and it is not marathon preparation. Marathon programs normally run 16 to 20 weeks and assume 3 to 6 months of running four times per week beforehand, so finishing 42.2 km after these 4 weeks cannot be prepared safely. The goal is kept unchanged as the client requested, and this plan states the gap honestly. The 4 weeks start the habit and the aerobic base (run/walk intervals, talk-test pacing), which is the first stage of any later marathon build. The client should check with a doctor before attempting any long event.

## Cardio Goal
- Target for these 4 weeks: build from 24 to 30 minutes of cardio per week, ending with 2 sessions of 15 minutes of run/walk intervals at talk-test pace, with at most about 10 percent weekly increase in duration (Runner's World advises avoiding more than about 10 percent volume increase).
- Reference public-health target: at least 150 minutes per week of moderate-intensity activity (WHO). Training cardio covers only 16 to 20 percent of that, so easy walking on non-training days is encouraged (WHO: all activity counts).
- Marathon readiness benchmark (not reachable in 4 weeks): 3 to 6 months of running four times per week, a comfortable 10 km, then a 16 to 20 week marathon block (Runner's World). The marathon distance is not attempted inside this program.

## Weekly Cardio Prescription
Durations include warm-up and cool-down. Cardio plus any strength or mobility block must stay within 20 minutes per day. No equipment is needed; run on streets, in parks, or on the spot indoors as a fallback. Every row's pattern adds up exactly to its stated duration (arithmetic shown in the type column). The final walk interval of each pattern serves as the cool-down.

| week | sessions | type | duration (min) | intensity | notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 2 (Mon, Fri) | Run/walk: 3 min walk + 4 x (1 min jog + 1 min walk) + 1 min walk. Arithmetic: 3 + 4 x 2 + 1 = 12; jog total 4 | 12 per session (24 per week) | Z1 walk, Z2 jog | Learn the pace; stop jogging if you cannot talk. Up to 8 min left for strength or mobility |
| 2 | 2 (Mon, Fri) | Run/walk: 3 min walk + 4 x (1.5 min jog + 1 min walk). Arithmetic: 3 + 4 x 2.5 = 13; jog total 6 | 13 per session (26 per week, +8%) | Z1 walk, Z2 jog | Jog slightly longer at the same easy effort. Up to 7 min left for strength or mobility |
| 3 | 2 (Mon, Fri) | Run/walk: 2 min walk + 4 x (2 min jog + 1 min walk). Arithmetic: 2 + 4 x 3 = 14; jog total 8 | 14 per session (28 per week, +8%) | Z1 walk, Z2 jog | Keep the walk breaks. Up to 6 min left for strength or mobility |
| 4 | 2 (Mon, Fri) | Run/walk: 3 min walk + 3 x (3 min jog + 1 min walk). Arithmetic: 3 + 3 x 4 = 15; jog total 9 | 15 per session (30 per week, +7%) | Z1 walk, Z2 jog | Checkpoint week: note the longest stretch you can jog while still able to talk. Up to 5 min left for strength or mobility |

Check of progression: weekly cardio 24, 26, 28, 30 minutes (increases of 8.3, 7.7 and 7.1 percent, all under 10 percent). Jog time per session rises 4, 6, 8, 9 minutes. Cardio plus strength/mobility block is 12 + 8, 13 + 7, 14 + 6, 15 + 5 = 20 minutes at most in every week.

Expected distance is roughly 1.5 to 2.5 km per session at beginner pace, so the 4 weeks total about 12 to 20 km, far from 42.2 km. Suggested next step after the program: a longer build starting from continuous 30-minute running, then a 16 to 20 week marathon block.

## Integration With Strength Training
- Days: Monday and Friday, the only training days (2 per week). Cardio is done in every session; there is no separate cardio-only day.
- Order: cardio first (warm-up included), then the short strength or mobility block, so running quality is not hurt by tired legs. Keep leg strength light because the gaps between sessions are only 2 to 3 days.
- Time budget: cardio of 12, 13, 14, 15 minutes per session plus the strength/mobility block must total at most 20 minutes; the remaining minutes (8, 7, 6, 5) are the limit for the program designer.
- Non-training days: easy walking up to 30 minutes is encouraged to move toward the WHO volume without adding running load.
