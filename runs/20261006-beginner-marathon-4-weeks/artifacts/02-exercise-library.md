## Substitutions
| exercise | easier option | harder option | why |
|---|---|---|---|
| Bodyweight Squat HD | Wall Squat | Prisoner Squat | wall support removes balance demand; hands behind head adds control demand |
| Reverse lunges | Bodyweight lunge HD | Single Leg RDL | stepping backward is usually gentler on the knee; single-leg hinge adds balance load |
| Good Morning | Single Leg Glute Bridge | Single Leg RDL | the bridge is lying down and stable; single-leg hinge is standing and unilateral |
| Plank | Bird Dog | Side Plank | Bird Dog is low load on all fours; side plank adds lateral demand |
| Reverse crunch | Bird Dog | Plank | Bird Dog is the gentlest option; plank needs less lower-back control than raising the legs and pelvis |
| Run | Walking | Run - Interval Training | walking is the lowest-impact option; intervals add intensity variation |
| Run - Interval Training | March or jog in place | Run | in-place jogging works in limited space or bad weather; continuous running is the longer effort |
| Leg Swings (Front–Back) | Knee to Chest Stretch | Standing Calf Stretch | the floor stretch needs no balance; the calf stretch targets a running-specific muscle with a wall |
