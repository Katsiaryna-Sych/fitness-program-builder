# Progress & Assessment
> Run: 20261002-home-beginner-strength · Step: progress · Owner: progress-planner · Attempt: 2

## Summary
Four baseline tests measure the primary goal (strength, shown as strength-endurance of the legs, chest/arms and trunk). They use only the floor, a wall, the sturdy household chair, the mat and a timer: a 30-second chair stand, a knee push-up max-rep test, a plank hold and a wall squat hold. They are done on the Saturday (a rest day) of week 1, week 6 (deload week) and week 12 (deload and retest week), so a test never sits next to a hard session. All three week numbers exist in the 12-week program. Every test ends at the first sign of form breakdown, not at exhaustion, which fits the program's "no sets to failure" rule (RIR 1-2 at most).

This attempt follows the revised program (attempt 2). Session lengths are now worst case A 27.9, B 27.3 and C 28.2 min against the 30-min limit. The warm-up is 3 min (Cow-cat plus Wall Squat holds), and rest is 60 s on main lifts and 45 s on holds. Some rep caps were lowered: Superman 8 reps at a 4 s hold, Single Leg Glute Bridge 8 per leg, Bird Dog 8 per side, lunges 10 per leg. Schedule (Mon/Wed/Fri), deload weeks 6 and 12 and the week-12 Friday retest of Session C are unchanged. Because every session is now within 1.8 min of the cap, the adjustment rules never add sets or lengthen rest. The old rule of 90 s rest after a failed checkpoint was removed, as it would push the sessions over 30 min.

No injuries or conditions were reported, so no test was replaced. The client should stop any test that causes pain and see a doctor if they have health doubts. Age, sex, height and weight were not provided, so pass criteria are relative to the client's own baseline, not population norms. Body weight and waist circumference are optional extras. The Friday week-12 Session C retest (1 set per exercise at RIR 1) is a training retest. The Saturday tests are the formal comparison.

## Baseline Tests
| test | protocol | what it measures | safe for client? | source |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 30-second chair stand | Use the sturdy chair (straight back, no armrests, seat about 43 cm / 17 in) pushed against a wall. Sit in the middle, feet flat, wrists crossed with hands on opposite shoulders. On "go", rise to full standing and sit back down for 30 s. Count full stands; a stand that is more than halfway up when 30 s ends counts. If the arms are needed to stand, stop and record 0, and keep using the Box squat for 2 more weeks. | Lower-body strength and endurance (quadriceps, glutes); mirrors the Box squat and Bodyweight Squat in Sessions A and B | Yes. No contraindicated movement reported; chair against a wall; stop if knee pain | [CDC STEADI 30-Second Chair Stand](https://www.cdc.gov/steadi/media/pdfs/STEADI-Assessment-30Sec-508.pdf) |
| Knee push-up max reps (modified push-up) | On the mat, kneel with hands shoulder-width apart and back straight. Lower the chest toward the floor to the same level every time, and press to straight arms. Count clean reps; a brief pause at the top is allowed, no resting the body on the floor. Stop at the first rep with sagging hips or short depth (do not go to exhaustion). Always use the knee version at all three checkpoints. If the client already does floor push-ups at week 6 or 12, do the knee test first, then record one floor push-up set after 5 min rest. | Upper-body push endurance (chest, front shoulders, triceps); mirrors knee push-ups and Push-Up in Sessions A and C | Yes. The form-based stop keeps it submaximal; no reported condition. The source's "until exhaustion" end rule is replaced by a form-based stop for a beginner | [Push-Up Test — Topend Sports](https://www.topendsports.com/testing/tests/home-pushup.htm); [ACSM CPT Chapter 12 — PT Pioneer](https://www.ptpioneer.com/personal-training/certifications/acsm/acsm-cpt-chapter-12/) |
| Plank hold | Forearms on the mat, body in one straight line from head to toe (or knees if using the knee version), head neutral, looking at the floor. Start the timer once the position is correct. Stop the clock when the hips drop or the back cannot stay straight. Record seconds. Use the same version at every checkpoint. | Trunk (core) endurance; mirrors the Plank in Session A and the Side Plank in Session C | Yes. The end rule is form-based; do not continue through sagging hips | [Plank Test — Topend Sports](https://www.topendsports.com/testing/tests/plank.htm) |
| Wall squat hold | Feet shoulder-width apart and about 60 cm (2 ft) from a wall, back flat against the wall, feet flat, knees and hips at about 90 degrees, knees above the ankles, arms across the chest. Timing starts when the position is reached and stops when it cannot be held. Stop at once if the knees hurt. | Isometric lower-body strength-endurance (quadriceps, glutes); same pattern as the Wall Squat in the warm-up of all sessions | Yes. An unloaded hold to 90 degrees, no deeper, is low risk | [Wall Squat Test — Topend Sports](https://www.topendsports.com/testing/tests/wall-squat.htm) |

Order and conditions: do the tests in the order above after the program's 3-min warm-up (Cow-cat 90 s, then 2 x 30 s Wall Squat holds). Rest 3-5 min between tests, test at the same time of day, with the same chair and mat. The tests take about 20 min in total. Do not do more than the four tests on that day. The Wall Squat holds in the warm-up are easy and shallow, so they do not affect the wall squat test, but keep the warm-up identical at all three checkpoints.

## Adjustment Rules
- Progression (consistent with the program's double-progression rule): if all sets of an exercise reach the top of the program's capped range (12 reps; 10 per leg for lunges; 8 per leg for Single Leg Glute Bridge; 8 per side for Bird Dog; 8 for Superman at a 4 s hold; or the top of the hold range) at RIR 2 or more for two sessions in a row, then move to the next variation listed in the program (for example Box squat to Bodyweight Squat HD, knee push-ups to Push-Up) and reset reps to 6-8. Do not raise reps above the program's caps; they are set to keep sessions within 30 min.
- Too easy: if the logged RPE is 6 or below on all exercises for two consecutive sessions in weeks 3-5 or 7-11, then start the 3-s lowering tempo early (not on the lunges, which stay at 2 s down) or move to the next variation. Do not add sets and do not shorten rest below 60 s (main lifts) or 45 s (holds), because Session C is already 28.2 min worst case.
- Stall: if reps do not increase for 2 weeks in a row on an exercise (not counting deload weeks 6 and 12), then stay on the same variation, repeat the previous week's reps and check sleep and rest. If the stall lasts 3 weeks, drop to the previous easier variation for 1 week, then rebuild.
- Too hard: if a set cannot be finished with clean form at 6 reps, or the RPE is 9-10 on the first set, then step down one variation (for example Push-Up to the chair incline or knee version) or drop one set from that exercise for the week.
- Fatigue: if rep counts fall for more than one week, or the client feels unusually tired or has aching joints, then bring forward an extra deload week (one set fewer per exercise, RIR 4), as in the program.
- Pain: if an exercise causes sharp or joint pain, or pain that lasts more than a few days, then stop that exercise, use the easier option from the exercise library, and see a health professional if the pain continues; do not train through it.
- Session over 30 min: if a session takes more than 30 min, then cut the second set of the last core exercise first (as in the program); never cut the warm-up.
- Missed sessions: if one session is missed, then shift the rest of the week by one day and never train on back-to-back days; if a full week is missed, then repeat the previous week's loads; if 2 or more weeks are missed, then restart at week 1 targets and retest.
- Checkpoint failure: if fewer than 3 of the 4 tests improved at week 6, then check attendance first. If fewer than 80% of sessions were done, continue unchanged and focus on attendance. If 80% or more were done, keep the current variations for weeks 7-8 (so the Single Leg Glute Bridge and Prisoner Squat swaps wait until the table criteria are met) and check sleep, food and rest. Do not lengthen rest between sets, because the revised sessions have only 1.8 min spare.
- Test safety: if a test cannot be done with clean form or causes pain, then stop, record the value reached, and do not repeat it that day. Replace it with an easier version (for example the knee plank) and keep that version for all later checkpoints.
