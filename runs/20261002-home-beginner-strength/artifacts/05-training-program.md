# Training Program
> Run: 20261002-home-beginner-strength · Step: program · Owner: program-designer · Attempt: 2

## Summary
This is a 12-week, 3-day full-body bodyweight strength program for a complete beginner who trains at home with a yoga mat and a sturdy household chair. There are three different sessions (A, B, C) on Monday, Wednesday and Friday. No preferred days were given, so these days were chosen to leave at least 48 h between sessions. Every session has a squat or lunge, a horizontal push, a floor-based pull or back extension, a hinge or bridge, and a core exercise. That makes 12 hard sets per session.

Attempt 2 changes how session time is worked out. Work time for each set is now the reps at the top of the range × seconds per rep (lowering + pause + lifting, plus any hold), counted for both sides where that applies, plus 15 s for each change between exercises. The time shown is the worst case: the hardest week and the hardest variation. The longest session is Session C at 28.2 min, which leaves 1.8 min under the 30-min limit (A 27.9, B 27.3 min). To fit this, the warm-up is 3 min, the cool-down is 2 min, and rest is 45 s on core and back-extension holds. The umbrella review found that rest length between sets does not change strength gains. Superman reps are capped at 8 when the hold is 4 s. The Single Leg Glute Bridge is capped at 8 reps per leg with a 1-s squeeze. Bird Dog is capped at 8 reps per side. The Hip Raise pause in Session C is 2 s. Lunges keep a fixed 2-s lowering.

Full-body training 2-3 days a week with 1-3 sets per exercise at 8-12 RM matches ACSM's advice for novices. ACSM's 2026 position stand finds that strength improves with 2-3 sets, full range of motion, the exercise done early in the session, and at least 2 sessions a week. The client has no weights, so the "heavier load" comes from harder bodyweight variations, slower lowering and single-leg work, with 6-12 reps and 1-3 reps in reserve (RIR). Weeks 6 and 12 are deload weeks, and week 12 ends with a retest. No safety or cardio plan exists for this run (needs_safety = no, needs_cardio = no), so there are no contraindications or cardio blocks. If the client has any doubts about their health, they should see a doctor before starting. They should stop any exercise that causes pain.

## Sessions
### Session A — Full body (squat / horizontal push emphasis)
**Warm-up (3 min):** Yoga exercise: Cow-cat ([1244](https://wger.de/en/exercise/1244/view-base)) slowly for 90 s (6-8 cycles), then Wall Squat ([718](https://wger.de/en/exercise/718/view-base)) 2 × 30 s easy holds at a shallow depth with 30 s rest between them = 90 s.
| # | exercise | sets | reps / time | rest | intensity | notes | source |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Box squat | 3 | 6-12 | 60 s | RIR per Progression Plan | Chair against a wall. Sit back until the glutes lightly touch the seat, pause 1 s, stand. Week 5 on (or once 3 × 12 with 3-s lowering is easy): swap to Bodyweight Squat HD (1312) | [wger 977](https://wger.de/en/exercise/977/view-base) |
| 2 | knee push-ups | 3 | 6-12 | 60 s | RIR per Progression Plan | Straight line from knees to head. Once 3 × 12 is reached, swap to Push-Up (1551) | [wger 1218](https://wger.de/en/exercise/1218/view-base) |
| 3 | Superman | 2 | weeks 1-8: 6-10 reps with a 2-3 s hold; weeks 9-11: 6-8 reps with a 4 s hold | 45 s | RIR per Progression Plan | Lift arms and legs together and breathe. Do not over-arch the neck. The rep cap keeps the set at 50 s or less | [wger 636](https://wger.de/en/exercise/636/view-base) |
| 4 | Hip Raise, Lying | 2 | 8-12 | 60 s | RIR per Progression Plan | 1 s up, 2 s squeeze at the top, 2 s down. Week 7 on: swap to Single Leg Glute Bridge (1740), 6-8 reps per leg, 1 s squeeze, both legs in each set | [wger 292](https://wger.de/en/exercise/292/view-base) |
| 5 | Plank | 2 | 20-45 s hold | 45 s | RIR per Progression Plan (stop with about 5-10 s left in the tank) | Ribs down, glutes tight. Drop to the knees if the hips sag | [wger 458](https://wger.de/en/exercise/458/view-base) |
**Cool-down (2 min):** Child's pose ([1002](https://wger.de/en/exercise/1002/view-base)) 60 s, then 60 s of slow breathing lying on the mat.
**Estimated duration:** 27.9 min worst case. Work per set is top-of-range reps × seconds per rep, using the hardest week:
- Box squat (week 4, 3 s down + 1 s pause + 1 s up = 5 s): 12 × 5 = 60 s. This is more than Bodyweight Squat HD from week 5 on (12 × 4 = 48 s), so 60 s is used.
- Push-up (week 4 on, 3 + 1 = 4 s): 12 × 4 = 48 s.
- Superman: weeks 1-8, 10 × (3 s hold + 2 s move) = 50 s; weeks 9-11, 8 × (4 + 2) = 48 s. 50 s is used.
- Bridge: Hip Raise, weeks 1-6, 12 × (1 + 2 + 2) = 60 s; Single Leg Glute Bridge, weeks 7-11, 8 reps/leg × 2 legs × (1 up + 1 squeeze + 2 down) = 64 s + 6 s to switch legs = 70 s. 70 s is used.
- Plank: 45 s.

Total: warm-up 180 s + Box squat 3 × (60 + 60) = 360 s + push-up 3 × (48 + 60) = 324 s + Superman 2 × (50 + 45) = 190 s + Single Leg Glute Bridge 2 × (70 + 60) = 260 s + Plank 2 × (45 + 45) = 180 s + 4 changes × 15 s = 60 s + cool-down 120 s = **1674 s = 27.9 min**.

Weeks 1-3 (3 s per rep, Hip Raise): 180 + 3 × (48 + 60) + 3 × (36 + 60) + 190 + 2 × (60 + 60) + 180 + 60 + 120 = 1582 s = 26.4 min.

**Estimated duration:** 27.3 min worst case (weeks 4-11). Work per set is top-of-range reps × seconds per rep:
- Squat (Bodyweight Squat HD or Prisoner Squat): 12 × 4 s = 48 s.
- Chair dips: 12 × 4 s = 48 s.
- Reverse Snow Angel: 12 × (2 + 2 s) = 48 s.
- Good Morning: 12 × (3 + 1 + 1 s) = 60 s.
- Bird Dog: 8 per side × 2 sides × (1 + 2 + 1 s) = 64 s.

Total: warm-up 180 s + squat 3 × (48 + 60) = 324 s + Chair dips 2 × (48 + 60) = 216 s + Reverse Snow Angel 3 × (48 + 45) = 279 s + Good Morning 2 × (60 + 60) = 240 s + Bird Dog 2 × (64 + 45) = 218 s + 4 changes × 15 s = 60 s + cool-down 120 s = **1637 s = 27.3 min**.

## Deload & Recovery
- **Rest days:** Tuesday, Thursday, Saturday and Sunday. Never do sessions on back-to-back days. If a session is missed, shift the rest of the week by one day instead of doubling up. An easy walk on rest days is optional.
- **Deload timing:** weeks 6 and 12 (one set fewer per exercise, RIR 4). Take an extra deload week early if joint aches, unusual tiredness or falling rep counts last more than a week.
- **Effort:** no sets to failure. The umbrella review found that training to failure is not needed for gains. Stopping short lowers fatigue and injury risk for a beginner.
- **Time cap:** if a session ever runs over 30 min (for example, longer rests), cut the second set of the last core exercise first. Do not cut warm-up time.
- **Sleep:** aim for 7 or more hours a night (CDC, adults 18-60). The CDC advises 7-9 hours for ages 61-64 and 7-8 hours for 65 and over.
- **Pain rule:** stop any exercise that causes sharp or joint pain, and use the easier option from the exercise library's Substitutions table. If pain lasts more than a few days, get it checked by a health professional.
- **Chair safety:** always put the chair against a wall for the box squat, chair dips and incline push-up.
