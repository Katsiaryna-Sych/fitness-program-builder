Chair notes (the client's sturdy household chair; wger lists these as bodyweight):
- Box squat (977): the chair serves as the box; sit back until the glutes lightly touch the seat, pause, then stand. Place the chair against a wall so it cannot slide.
- Bulgarian split squats left (988): the rear foot rests on the chair seat (wger instructions: "Stand tall in front of a chair"). Id 989 is the right-side mirror and is done as the same exercise for the other leg. Chair against a wall; use a hand on a wall or table for balance at first.
- Chair dips (1774): hands on the front edge of the chair, as described by wger. Chair against a wall; keep hips close to the chair and do not go deeper than a right angle at the elbow.
- Push-Up (1551): optional incline variation with hands on the chair seat (chair against a wall) as an easier regression, then progress to the floor. wger has no separate incline push-up entry, so this is a variation of Push-Up and not an extra row.
- Step-ups: wger has no step-up entry, so they are not in the library. If the planner wants a step-up-like pattern, use Bulgarian split squats or Reverse lunges.
- Superman and Reverse Snow Angel are the floor-based pulling substitutes for a client without a bar, band or weights.
